I am a Senior Staff Applied Cryptographer on the Applied Cryptography team at [Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com/).
I research and design cryptographic protocols (zero-knowledge proofs, MPC, threshold signing, and related primitives), prove their security, and help turn them into production systems—including Coinbase’s core cryptography library [`cb-mpc`](https://github.com/coinbase/cb-mpc) and the MPC framework that secures Coinbase Wallet.

Previously, I was a Senior Cryptography Engineer at [Polymath](http://polymath.network/), working on ZKP-based solutions for Polymesh, and before that a Team Lead / Senior Software Engineer on the Private Cloud team at [Cisco](https://www.cisco.com/).

I received my PhD in Computer Science from [University of Calgary](https://www.ucalgary.ca/icis/) on
[Design and Implementation of Maliciously Secure Two-Party Computation](https://prism.ucalgary.ca/handle/11023/3899).

I deeply care about security and software engineering, in particular how to create novel and practical software
solutions and protect the privacy of the user without asking them to put a huge amount of trust on third party companies.

The contents of this blog are my personal opinions and does not necessarily reflect the opinions of my employers.
Moreover, as I learn more and evolve, my opinions and world views changes evolve (hopefully for the better).
